Frequently asked questions

About the Markdown Converter

What is the difference between Markdown and an MD file?

Markdown is a lightweight markup format. An MD file stores Markdown content with a .md or .markdown extension.

Can I convert AI-generated content directly?

Yes. Paste structured content from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Grok, or another AI assistant.

Which office files can be converted to Markdown?

DOCX, Excel, CSV, and PDFs with selectable text are supported. Scanned PDFs require OCR.

What happens to images inside a PDF?

The current converter extracts selectable text and asks you to review pages containing images or graphics.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Conversion runs locally in your browser. Avoid using any online tool for information that must never be exposed.
