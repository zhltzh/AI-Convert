A Free iLovePDF Alternative for Document Conversion

Browser-based, no upload, no sign-up — when that trade-off is the right one.

iLovePDF is a solid toolbox for editing PDFs you already have. Aixuno starts one step earlier: it turns Markdown, AI answers and research notes into polished Word, PDF, HTML or Excel files — and it does the whole job inside your browser, so no file ever leaves your device.

Where each tool draws its line

The two tools overlap less than the search results suggest.

  • iLovePDF edits existing PDFs: merge, split, compress, rotate, convert to Office formats. Files are uploaded to its servers for processing.
  • Aixuno creates documents from text: Markdown and AI answers become Word, PDF, HTML or Excel. Processing runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded, ever.

If your day starts with an existing PDF you need to slice, iLovePDF stays a reasonable pick. If your day starts with text — a ChatGPT answer, meeting notes, a research summary — that needs to become a document, Aixuno removes the upload entirely.

How privacy differs in practice

The difference is structural, not a policy promise. When you use a server-side converter, every document you process transits and rests on infrastructure you do not control. With Aixuno, the conversion code runs on your machine: closing the tab ends the story. There is no queue, no retention window, no upload log — because there is no upload.

Which one fits your work

  • Choose iLovePDF when the input is a finished PDF and you need page-level surgery: merge, split, compress, rotate.
  • Choose Aixuno when the input is text — an AI answer, Markdown notes, a report draft — and the output is a document someone will read or edit.
  • Use both when a draft grows up in Aixuno and the finished PDF needs a final trim.

Frequently asked questions

Can Aixuno fully replace iLovePDF?

No. They solve different halves of the document workflow. Aixuno creates documents from text; iLovePDF edits PDFs that already exist.

Why would someone skip iLovePDF?

File privacy is the most common reason — uploads sit on third-party servers — followed by predictable free usage without account limits.

Is Aixuno free?

Yes. Every converter is free, with no sign-up and no usage caps.

Is the output quality better than iLovePDF?

For creating documents from Markdown and AI answers, quality is at least as good. The structural difference is privacy: nothing is stored because nothing is uploaded.
