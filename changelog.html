Product updates

Changelog

A record of material changes to product capabilities, privacy practices, language support, and page structure.

  • 2026-11-01
    Keyword-mapped page titles

    Homepages and every language edition of the tool pages now lead with the action a visitor searches for, plus a free or local-processing qualifier.

  • 2026-11-01
    Share controls

    Guides and tool pages gained copy-link, X and Weibo sharing. Copy falls back to the legacy clipboard path when the Clipboard API is blocked.

  • 2026-11-01
    Full-answer guides

    All 18 guides now cover where the tool fits, where it does not, comparisons with other routes, and troubleshooting. Guides and tool pages link to each other in both directions.

  • 2026-11-01
    International signals completed

    hreflang groups rebuilt so every language edition points at all the others, 45 pages carry FAQPage markup, and 18 articles declare author, publisher and dates.

  • 2026-10-31
    Open Graph and Twitter cards

    82 pages gained social cards backed by a generated 1200×630 image, with og:locale matched to each language edition.

  • 2026-10-31
    AI crawler access

    robots.txt now names thirteen AI crawlers explicitly, and 18 migrated stub pages were marked noindex while keeping their canonical target.

  • 2026-10-31
    One h1 per page

    Preview headings are demoted and restyled so each page exposes a single h1 to crawlers; the homepage draft is now shared across all four export modes.

  • 2026-10-30
    Download reliability

    When a browser swallows a download, a manual save link stays available. The floating tray appears only when there is something to download.

  • 2026-10-29
    Blank PDF fix

    Exports capture from the top of the page, so scrolled documents no longer produce blank pages.

  • 2026-10-29
    Local PDF assets

    pdf.js standard fonts and character maps are served from this site instead of a CDN.

  • 2026-10-29
    Performance and accessibility

    Stylesheets consolidated, layout shift from the JS-rendered workspace removed, contrast and tap targets raised.

  • 2026-10-29
    Site foundations

    Every page gained a favicon and declares its charset before any other tag.

Aixuno · Improve continuously, without overstating what is not built.
