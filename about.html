About

Make useful AI content part of your work.

Aixuno is a collection of browser-based conversion tools. We help turn AI answers into documents and knowledge assets that can be edited, shared, published, and reused.

Why we exist

AI can produce a lot of useful content, but it often remains trapped in a chat window. We reduce the friction between getting an answer and putting it to work—without requiring an account, uploading sensitive content, or learning complex software.

Our principles

  • Local first: core conversions happen in the user's browser.
  • Task first: every tool exists to complete a clear job.
  • Honest and transparent: we only describe capabilities and limits that can be verified.
  • Localized: each language version is adapted to its users, not merely translated.

Current scope

Aixuno currently supports Markdown to Word, Markdown to PDF, Excel/CSV to Markdown, and Markdown to HTML for websites, blogs, and CMS publishing workflows. The product is actively improving; material changes are recorded in the changelog.
