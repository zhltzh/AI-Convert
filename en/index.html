FAQ

About Aixuno

We make AI-generated content easier to keep, use, and share.

What is Aixuno?

Aixuno is a set of browser-based tools for turning AI-generated content into Word, PDF, Markdown, and other useful formats.

Is my content private?

Yes. Conversion happens locally in your browser. Your files are not uploaded to a server.

Which AI platforms are supported?

You can use content from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Grok, and more.
