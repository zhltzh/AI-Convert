How to Export an AI Answer

Choose Word, PDF, or Markdown based on what you need next.

A useful AI answer should not disappear in a chat history. Save it in an appropriate format so it can be edited, shared, or reused as knowledge.

Choose the right output format

  • Word: best when you need to edit, comment on, or circulate a document.
  • PDF: best for a stable version you want to share, print, or archive.
  • Markdown: best for notes, documentation, and knowledge bases.

A simple workflow

  1. Copy the answer from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, or another AI assistant.
  2. Choose the output format that fits your next task.
  3. Review the result and download or copy it into your workflow.
Aixuno processes content locally. Your source text and files are not sent to an application server.

Frequently asked question

Which format is most flexible?
Markdown is easy to reuse in notes and documentation. Word is usually better when other people need to edit the result.
